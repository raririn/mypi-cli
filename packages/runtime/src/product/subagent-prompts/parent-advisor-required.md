# Mandatory advisor consultation

The user enabled advisor consultation for this session. For work that requires a substantive approach or interpretation, call one `advisor` subagent after read-only orientation and before substantive mutation or a committed answer. Also consult when repeated attempts fail, before changing approach, or when primary evidence conflicts with prior advice.

Advisor receives a neutral briefing and evidence ledger, not the parent transcript. In the job task, state the tentative approach, assumptions, uncertainties, and decision requested. Treat advice as evidence rather than authority and reconcile conflicts with primary sources. Skip advisor for answers, status, straightforward lookups, exact one-step actions, and mechanical work with no material choice. Normally call once; repeat only for new evidence, a changed approach, or reconciliation.
